Help

    

      
Record a screen or window

      
    
        
  1. Open Choose what to record and select a screen/window thumbnail.
    2. 
        
  2. Choose capture mode (screen/window, region, or all displays). In Recording Controls, select Video + Audio or Audio Only in the compact mode selector, then use the blue Start recording button and turn Webcam overlay on or off with the Apple-style switch. Microphone is enabled by default; use the mic icon beside Mic in Audio check for a quick mute/unmute before recording. Use Recording setup for audio/video quality and Recording options for filename, folder, countdown, cursor, and keystrokes. Microphone noise removal defaults to Enhanced voice with adaptive fan/air removal; use Strong when the fan or air flow is very loud.
    3. 
        
  3. Click Start recording. While recording, use Pause/Resume to temporarily stop capture and continue in the same output, or Stop recording to finish.
    4. 
        
  4. Video recordings save as MP4 and audio-only recordings save as M4A. New media saves to the selected Recording folder; the default is Movies/PulseStudio on macOS or Videos/PulseStudio on Windows. Automatic transcripts, snapshots and default exports use this folder too. Saved custom folders remain available, and older recordings directly in Movies or Videos stay in Playback.
    5. 
      

      
Readiness
Before recording, check the readiness strip above Recording Controls. It flags missing sources/regions, microphone/camera permissions, system-audio mode, and low recording-folder storage.

      
Audio-only recording
Choose Audio Only in the Recording Controls mode switch when you do not need video. Microphone and system audio continue to work, while video-only quality, FPS, cursor, keystroke, and webcam controls are disabled.

      
Filename templates
Use tokens {date}, {time}, {datetime}, {source}, {mode}, and {type}. The filename preview updates before recording and duplicate names are made unique automatically.

      
Webcam overlay
In Recording Controls, click the Webcam overlay switch to add or remove your camera with one click. When enabled, choose its camera, position, size, and circular or rounded shape directly beneath the switch.

      
Recording recovery
Active recording chunks and session metadata are journaled while recording. If the app exits before Stop is pressed, relaunch PulseStudio; the unfinished source stays protected and a compact Recover option appears. Recovery runs only when you choose Recover, can be stopped, and never blocks a new recording.

      
Selected-application audio
Choose a window thumbnail and select Selected application only under System audio. On macOS this uses ScreenCaptureKit. On supported 64-bit Windows builds it uses WASAPI process-loopback and captures the selected process plus child processes while excluding unrelated application audio.

      
Meeting audio on speakers
Whenever the microphone is enabled, PulseStudio automatically uses the strongest supported speaker echo cancellation and checks that it is active. This works independently of the Computer Audio setting. Choose Off, Standard, Enhanced voice, or Strong under Recording setup → Noise removal; Enhanced voice is a good starting point for fan or air noise. Results depend on your microphone, speaker volume, and room. Available microphone and computer-audio source tracks are kept locally with the recording for recovery and troubleshooting; they follow rename and Trash.

      
Snapshots, bookmarks & editing
Use Snapshot while recording or the camera button in Playback. Use Bookmark while recording to mark an important moment. Playback shows bookmarks as timeline markers. Open Bookmarks beneath the player for Previous, Add, Delete, Next, or View / edit bookmarks. Add bookmark opens the optional marker-text editor above the waveform; you have 3 seconds to begin entering a note. Press B for the same action. Named markers stay visible on the waveform, appear briefly over the video when playback reaches them, and can be double-clicked on the waveform to edit their text. Snapshots save beside recordings. For a simple trim, adjust the waveform-backed start/end handles and save a trimmed copy. To remove several unwanted sections, select each range, choose + Add selected range, then Save cleaned copy. All editing is non-destructive and the original recording is never overwritten. Export beneath the player includes full audio as MP3 or M4A, transcript TXT, subtitles SRT, and the interval between bookmarks.
Export between bookmarks
In Playback → Trim & cuts, choose two saved bookmarks under Export between bookmarks. Choose Clip · MP4, Audio · M4A, TXT transcript, or SRT transcript, then Export interval. Only the section between those bookmarks is saved. The start must precede the end. Audio-only recordings offer Audio, TXT and SRT. If a transcript sentence crosses a bookmark or usable timings are missing, the app transcribes just that interval. SRT times start at the exported interval. The original stays selected and unchanged.
Transcript search

      
After a recording is saved, its transcript is generated automatically in the background. The local speech model is reused between jobs without reducing the existing quality settings; first loading and long recordings can still take time. Search inside Transcript & subtitles; matches jump the video directly to that moment. Choose Raw (default), Speakers, or Timecoded. Speaker view uses local speaker detection and starts a new paragraph when the detected speaker changes. The transcript remains read-only, but you can copy, export, or turn on CC.

      
Library search
The Playback search field searches filenames, categories, and locally saved transcript text. Open Sort & filter next to the media selector for date, duration, name, or file size in either direction, and categories. The full-height divider resizes the library. Your choice is remembered and Previous/Next follows the displayed order.

      
Chapters, meeting summary & action items
After transcription, Playback automatically creates local chapters, an extractive meeting summary, and likely action items from the transcript. Chapter, summary, and action-item timestamps jump directly to the matching moment. Meeting insights are cached locally and can be regenerated without an API key.

      
Keyboard shortcuts
Command/Ctrl+Alt+R Record/Stop · P Pause/Resume · B Bookmark · S Snapshot · M Full/Mini Controller. Press F1 or ? anytime for the complete shortcut reference. A shortcut may be unavailable if the operating system or another app already owns it.

      
Playback & subtitles

      
Quiet Studio keeps the library, player and review tools together with fewer repeated buttons. Use Transcript, Insights, Trim & cuts, and Timeline in the inspector. In narrower windows, the inspector moves below the player. The Raw transcript / Speakers / Timecoded selector changes the transcript view; speaker corrections appear in Speakers. Copy, export and further transcript options sit beside the selector. Transcript options include retries for transcription and speaker detection. Use arrows, Home and End to navigate menus, or Escape to close a menu.

      
Open the Playback tab, choose a recorded clip, and turn on CC in the player when the transcript is ready. Use the recording’s ··· menu to rename its media and companion files together or show it in its folder. Each library row has a ··· menu for Rename, Category, and Move to Trash; the star marks a Favorite. The library’s ··· menu includes Select recordings, Refresh, Open folder, Change folder, and Add category. Moving a recording to Trash still asks for confirmation and includes its companion files.

      
Full View and Mini Controller

      
Click Open Mini Controller above the Record or Playback workspace to keep recording controls on screen. Full View includes your existing recording setup, options, Playback, editing, transcription, and App & AI tools. The Mini window keeps its original compact size and is recording-only. Choose Video + Audio with the camera icon or Audio Only with the waveform icon before you start; hover over either icon for its hint. During recording, the small mode icon confirms your choice next to the timer. Microphone mute, Pause/Resume, Bookmark, Stop, and live audio meters remain close at hand. Use the expand icon in Mini's toolbar to open Full View for the rest of your tools. Adding a bookmark replaces the lower meter and status area with its optional text editor while Stop stays available. You have 3 seconds to start entering a note. Once you start typing, the editor stays open until you save it with Save or Enter, or dismiss it.

      
Saving after Stop
PulseStudio finalizes the MP4 or M4A after you stop. Large recordings and slow disks can still take time, while progress shows the current stage. Audio checks now read the media header instead of decoding the entire recording. Once the recording is saved safely, library refresh and automatic transcription continue in the background without holding the recorder’s Save state.

      
Appearance

      
New installs start in light appearance. Use the sun/moon icon to switch between light and dark; your choice is remembered. Themes in Full View keeps both Classic and Studio available. Mini's Start and Stop buttons use a soft blue surface; the button label and recording status tell you which action is available. The Pearl Screen app icon uses a pearl tile with a blue monitor and waveform. On macOS, its Dock symbol and lower tile shading change to a stronger aqua while recording, including pauses, and returns to blue when the recording stops. It uses no red dot or flashing animation.

      
Window transparency

      
In Mini View, drag the small transparency bar from 0% to 75%, or use the arrow keys while the bar is focused. 0% is fully opaque and 75% is the most transparent. Your choice is remembered. Full View stays fully opaque and has no transparency control.

      
Control hints

      
Rest the pointer on a control for a moment, or focus it with the keyboard, to see a short hint. Hints appear outside the Mini window so they do not cover its timer or buttons. Clicking, dragging, scrolling, switching windows, or pressing Escape dismisses the hint.

      
Mac Dock name
The prepared Mac runtime shows Pulse Studio in the Dock. The launcher preserves the existing host code identity; macOS privacy settings may still label it Electron. A sealed or unfamiliar runtime keeps its original display name so naming cannot block launch.
macOS permissions

      
If recording or microphone capture is blocked, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and enable Screen & System Audio Recording and Microphone access for the app requesting access, then reopen PulseStudio. The shared package opens with PulseStudio.app on Mac or Start PulseStudio - Windows.bat on Windows.

      
Windows permissions

      
If microphone capture is blocked, check Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone. System audio uses Windows loopback capture.

      
Diagnostic logs
PulseStudio keeps rotating troubleshooting logs in the child logs folder. Open About & Diagnostics → Open logs when you need to inspect or share a recording failure. The log contains runtime, capture, encoder, recovery, CPU/memory, and error details; it does not contain recorded audio or video content.
